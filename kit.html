Product overview

Lead Recovery Kit

A complete operating system for recovering missed calls and reactivating dead CRM leads in HVAC, plumbing, roofing, dental, landscaping, and similar local service trades.

Who it’s for

Owners and ops leads at 1–20 truck (or chair) local service businesses who know leads are leaking but don’t have a documented recovery process. Also useful for agencies who install lead systems for clients.

What you get

  • Missed-call recovery SOP — response windows, ownership, after-hours rules.
  • SMS/email script pack — first touch, estimate follow-up, re-engage, seasonal, review ask.
  • CRM hygiene checklist — statuses, required fields, weekly cleanup ritual.
  • Automation outline — n8n/Make steps from missed call → SMS → task → alert.
  • 30-day implementation plan — sequenced rollout so the team actually adopts it.
  • DFY pricing guidance — how to sell setup + retainer if you productize this yourself.

How delivery works

You’ll receive the Markdown + printable HTML/PDF kit. Checkout is on Gumroad — you get instant download after payment.
